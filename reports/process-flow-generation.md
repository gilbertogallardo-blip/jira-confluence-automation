# Process Flow

## Current State
1. Receive Jira request for a business change or gap.
2. Functional lead reviews the request and confirms the business objective.
3. Analyst captures the existing process steps, actors, and system touchpoints.
4. Current-state workflow is documented with approvals, handoffs, and exceptions.
5. Reviewers identify process gaps, missing context, and dependency risks.
6. Documentation is returned for clarification or approval before transformation work begins.

## Target State
1. Normalize the Jira request into a consistent internal business object.
2. Validate required fields, process context, and related dependencies.
3. Define the target business process with redesigned steps and approval controls.
4. Map each target step to actors, systems, and expected outcomes.
5. Compare current and target states to highlight removed, added, or reordered actions.
6. Finalize the reviewed process flow for downstream specification generation and validation.

## Gap Summary
- The current-state flow is based on existing manual and system-driven steps.
- The target-state flow adds clearer ownership, approval gates, and process sequencing.
- The main delta is the improved control and traceability of the future-state business process.
