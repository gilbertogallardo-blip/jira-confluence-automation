# How to Fill Out the Status Report

1. Enter the project name, reporting week, and your name.
2. In the Overview section, note whether the project is on track, at risk, or delayed.
3. Under Accomplishments, list completed milestones, tasks, and wins from the week.
4. Under Current Risks and Blockers, include any issues, delays, or dependencies that could affect delivery.
5. Under Tasks for Next Week, list the upcoming work that needs to happen.
6. Under Dependencies and Decisions Needed, capture items awaiting input, approval, or support from others.
7. In the Summary section, describe overall progress, any major concerns, and your recommendation for the next step.
8. Keep the report concise, factual, and focused on scope, schedule, risk, and decisions.
