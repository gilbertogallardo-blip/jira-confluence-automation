# Jira and Confluence Automation Ideas for a Manager

## 1. Automated Status Report Generator
**Problem it solves:** Managers often spend time manually gathering updates from multiple Jira issues, sprint boards, and Confluence pages to create weekly status reports. This automation reduces reporting effort and helps ensure reports are accurate and up to date.

**Data it needs:**
- Jira project and sprint data
- Issue status, assignee, priority, due dates, and completion percentage
- Confluence page links or project updates
- Team velocity and sprint burndown information
- Manager-defined reporting period and template

## 2. Release Readiness and Risk Alert Workflow
**Problem it solves:** Teams often miss blockers or late-risk items before a release, causing delays and confusion. This automation flags Jira issues with risks, dependencies, or overdue tasks and posts a summary to Confluence so managers can act before launch.

**Data it needs:**
- Jira issue states, labels, blockers, dependencies, and release versions
- Due dates and overdue task information
- Team or component ownership metadata
- Confluence page for release notes or launch checklists
- Rules for risk thresholds, such as blocked issues or unresolved dependencies

## 3. Team Performance and Capacity Dashboard Sync
**Problem it solves:** Managers struggle to keep a clear view of team workload, capacity, and delivery trends across multiple projects. This automation consolidates Jira and Confluence information into a dashboard that highlights overload, underutilization, and missed commitments.

**Data it needs:**
- Jira sprint capacity data, story points, and issue assignments
- Time tracking or effort estimates
- Team membership and role mapping
- Historical sprint performance data
- Confluence project summaries or planning notes
- Dashboard filters by team, date range, or project
